01

Pulp & Paper

I study the pulp and paper industry from upstream to downstream, including the relationship between raw materials, process parameters, and product quality.

  • Raw material preparation, pulp formation, and pulp screening and bleaching.
  • Stock preparation and sheet formation on the paper machine.
  • Pressing, drying, coating, and finishing and rewinding processes.
  • Quality control, process efficiency, and environmental management.
Learned through coursework and internship experience.
02

Data Analyst

I'm developing my data analysis skills with Microsoft Excel, from data preparation to presenting analysis results.

  • Data cleaning: handling duplicates, blanks, and inconsistent formats.
  • Data processing with Sort, Filter, text functions, and date functions.
  • Logical and aggregate formulas such as IF, SUMIFS, and COUNTIFS, plus lookups with XLOOKUP.
  • Summarizing with PivotTables, chart visualization, and dashboard building.
Focused on accurate processing and clear data presentation.
